=======================================================
November 7, 2026. This is a navigation aid, not evidence of a new benchmark.
Keep the full objective: usable self-hosted inference on the P100A, targeting
20 tokens/second, with no CPU-reference inference in Work. Validation remains
separate; preserve the working system and do not discard existing work.

Milestone 2: make a go/no-go decision on one candidate
-----------------------------------------------------
Candidate: converted BFP4/BFP8 FFN weights and native card operations. A
single BFP4 band took roughly 227k card cycles including GDDR and compute,
versus roughly 643k in a prior Q4 diagnostic, but inputs and representations
are not an equivalent-accuracy paired benchmark. Whole-model quality is not
established; one-token hybrid quality tests are warnings, not a pass.
Source inspection also confirms the recorded Q4 and BFP4 timing runs did not
use the same input vector: the Q4 exporter uses a (column*19 + block*11)
pattern, while the BFP4 exporter uses an (input*13) pattern. Thus even their
input data are not paired; do not quote their cycle ratio as an A/B speedup.
See pioneer_native_compact_q4_stream_export.cpp and
pioneer_bfp4_real_band_export.cpp for the exact BF16 encodings.

Before further full-model integration, define a model-relevant accuracy gate,
measure representative real weights and prompts against the Q4 reference,
and establish a matched on-card operation comparison including data movement.
Report the results and accept, improve, or abandon the candidate. Avoid
claiming model speed from band cycles or treating cache preparation as a gain.
Relevant evidence: benchmarks/qwen38-bfp4-dram-real-band-physical-20261106.txt
and benchmarks/qwen38-bfp-hybrid-cpu-quality-20261106.txt.

Milestone 3: prove full-model improvement
-----------------------------------------
Only after the candidate passes its gate, connect it safely to the real
model and compare matched full-27B requests, including correctness, generation
tokens/second, loading time, repeatability and safe driver restoration.
An isolated operation or a successful build does not complete this milestone.

Milestone 4: usable Assistant
-----------------------------
Keep a qualified model loaded across requests, expose the proven Work mode in
Assistant, verify load/unload and real use, and measure warm response latency.
Do not replace the working Assistant with an unqualified experiment.

At each milestone report: change, test scope, measured result, quality limits,
decision, and next action. More detailed history remains in P100A_WORK_PLAN.txt,
P100A_CURRENT_STATUS.txt and benchmarks/. Do not delete the evidence.
