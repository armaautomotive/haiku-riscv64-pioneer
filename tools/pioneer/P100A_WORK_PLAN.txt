P100A / Qwen 27B: four compact goals
====================================
Updated November 6, 2026. Keep the full goal: usable self-hosted Qwen3.8-27B
Q4_K_M on P100A, as fast as safely possible, targeting 20 tokens/sec.
Work has no CPU inference, CPU-reference validation or fallback; validation
is separate. Preserve Haiku boot,64 CPUs,full RAM and Assistant, and push
tested checkpoints. 20tok/s is unmet and unproven.

Baseline
--------
The full model runs on card, including attention/state. One short counting
response measured 0.17287tok/s; this is not a usable service or broad quality
test. Setup was 25.65 minutes and weights were released at END. Current
stable041/gen10 is a rollback driver, not the experimental fast engine.

----------------------------------------------------------
Integrate only an accepted engine. Compare against the prior fast path on
