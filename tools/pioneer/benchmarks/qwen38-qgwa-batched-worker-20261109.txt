QGWA batched input: isolated compiled worker simulation, 2026-11-09

The per-band batched BFP8 input leaf is linked into a separate QGWA worker.
Its boot and job identities differ from QGW8 and QGW9; it has no ROOT,
native-driver or Work selector. The worker and receipt account for the
batched NoC command and counters. The BFP4 path remains unchanged.

Two builds have matching ELF SHA-256:
e0d7bb5a1434181a8560867f1bfee701a7787a878dff780bce215adf79f2fc47
An ordinary QGW8 rebuild still matches its original ELF:
d28edbd5cf25b8184d29ddf189b74efe843d934cd8ebb628976ed3331823897d

ASan/UBSan simulations of emitted worker instructions passed synthetic
head jobs of 1, 2, 3, and 8 bands, FFN widths 5120 and 17408, 2048
single-bit receipt mutations, rejection of malformed jobs and mismatched
boot markers. Corrupting the local input between bands was repaired by the
next re-fetch in this simulator. Three private real-head bands on each of
three simulated peers matched 576 output words exactly. Histories were
injected; this is not a 64-layer model test. QGW8 and QGW9 regression
simulations passed. The simulated two-band head issued 322 operand reads
versus QGW8's 640 and took 214538 versus 232884 simulated RV32 steps.
These are NOT physical timings, throughput, full-model output quality, or
evidence that either simulated instruction count predicts a speedup.

This design still needs physical same-band input ownership, hardware NoC
ordering and coherence, full worker scheduling, bounded recovery, complete
model correctness and end-to-end performance qualification. No device
authority, driver installation, Assistant selection or Work-mode change is
granted by this result. The existing live QGW8 diagnostic was not disturbed.

Reproduce the simulation after building the two isolated images:
python3 tools/pioneer/tests/run_pioneer_q38_qgwa_batch_worker_tests.py ABS_BUILD_A ABS_BUILD_B ABS_BATCH_LEAF ABS_OLD_WORKER ABS_PRIVATE_EXPORT

Follow-up simulated-fabric check
--------------------------------
The initial mock completed partial input bursts immediately. It now delays
completion, queues all three requests for the 17408-wide input, and asserts
that the simulated worker does not touch its input scratch before completion.
An injected permanently stalled input must fail without scattering or
retrying. The full leaf suite passed 238 geometry cases and 40 refusals; the
compiled QGWA head/FFN and private-data simulations passed again. Its
two-band head now executes 214622 simulated steps due to the added delay;
this is a mock schedule, not a physical performance measurement. The first
full leaf regression also exposed an outdated expected compiler-error string
after the new worker role was added; that test expectation was corrected and
the complete suite rerun successfully with unchanged source. These checks
still do not model the actual hardware's memory ordering or source lifetime.
