Recurrent upload session control, offline simulation

The distinct recurrent Begin/Seal inspection control now checks root/team,
same cookie, runtime owner, reload generation, span owner, pre-cohort state,
and exclusion of legacy hybrid/QGW8/QGWD receipts. One prepared span passed
through the actual fast span-service core on a simulated endpoint; the
remaining 21,474 spans were recorded synthetically. The actual span close
service and 21,475-span receipt seal/revalidation passed. Lost Begin or Seal
replies quarantined the simulated owner and prevented retry; failed incoming
copy, foreign team/cookie and competing receipt were refused. Existing QGW8
and QGWD simulated controls passed regression tests.

This control has no ioctl or real dispatch registration and is forbidden in
kernel/native, Work and CPU-reference roles. It does not authenticate actual
model data, establish native resource lifetimes or permit device execution.
It is not a performance measurement.
