corrects its tested four-lane shift. QGWC is a separate worker that uses
that setting and a distinct QGWC job identity; the ROOT now has a matching,
separately gated QGWC image inspection build. This is NOT a Work selector.

synthetic simulation: 72 recurrent/dense projection jobs, the 973-job head
plan, an actual worker/leaf head execution and receipt, and the linked ROOT
head shape, packing and receipt checks. Invalid grammars and receipt mutations
were refused. The separate transition test also exercised QGWC BFP4 -> dense
attention -> BFP4 under the hypothetical SrcA address model. A QGW8 ROOT
regression build retained the original executable section hashes.

These are synthetic tests, not a full Qwen inference or P100A benchmark. The
coordinator build does not itself provide a native owner or model launch path.

and guarded full-model owner, followed by real token correctness and speed.
