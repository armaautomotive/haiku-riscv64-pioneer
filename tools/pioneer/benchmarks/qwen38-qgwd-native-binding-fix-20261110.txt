QGWD upload receipt to token-memory owner binding, 2026-11-10

The QGWD control creates its receipt with the PioneerStoreContext as its
context. The token-memory inspection previously tried to revalidate that
receipt using the different PioneerQ38DispatchLease address. Its synthetic
test had accidentally used the dispatch lease as the receipt's context too,
masking the mismatch. That interface could not have accepted the real
control-created receipt.

The journal now records the receipt's actual context separately from the
dispatch cohort. It checks the same sealed receipt, original context, cookie,
ledger, hashes, team, bank proof, and driver generation while also binding
the exact worker cohort. The fixture uses distinct context/cohort objects and
checks rejection of a substituted context, cohort, or generation before L1
I/O, then exercises the actual QGWD journal and dense-manifest path.

This is an inspection-profile integration correction. It does not enable a
native driver, establish a real card launch, validate a full model, or measure
inference speed.
