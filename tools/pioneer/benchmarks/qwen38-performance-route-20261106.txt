Decision
---------
Do not continue blind BF16 Q4 arithmetic replacements. Native113/114/115
are correct for their fixed cases but slower than their paired old path;
the complete BF16 engine110 is already over2x slower than earlier92.
Use the faster original-layout vector92 engine as the next profiling
subject, preserving its actual math, all learned tensors and row cover.
There is NO evidenced 116x improvement path yet. A peak-bandwidth estimate
does not supply one. Return to measured controller/worker attribution before
another implementation, not another cache rewrite or UI feature.

Assumptions: one pass/token with no retained weight reuse or extra fetches;
no embedding lookup, KV/GDN state, activations, protocol or cache-correction

Code trace / one next measurement
---------------------------------
Projection::Run separates preparation, dispatch, gather/wait and finish.
Preparation also admits worker histories; gather includes completion,
source/counter receipts and output collection. Some duplicate audits have
ALREADY been removed/deferred in tested variants; do not redo them.
The full-model global worker uses119 compute participants plus the root;
full-card execution does not imply the compute units are kept efficiently
busy. Tenstorrent documents separate Tensix instruction streams and a
paired unpack/math pipeline; that is an architectural option, not evidence
that our current kernel is limited by thread count or will gain116x:
https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-isa-documentation/blob/main/BlackholeA0/TensixTile/BabyRISCV/PushTensixInstruction.md
https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-metal/blob/main/tt_metal/tt-llk/tt_llk_blackhole/llk_lib/llk_math_matmul.h

NEXT: one distinct, qualified fast-engine ROOT phase profile. Keep vector92
worker/math bytes unchanged; instrument the complete ORIGINAL-layout root
to split preparation/dispatch/gather/finish and outside-call work during
the actual post-prompt window. Preserve the original851 source,8192context,
two recurrent slots, full vocabulary/row cover, ownership and clean END.
Do not use BF16 phase percentages as its result or reuse a spent stage.
Finish with measured dominant cost and ONE supported structural change
