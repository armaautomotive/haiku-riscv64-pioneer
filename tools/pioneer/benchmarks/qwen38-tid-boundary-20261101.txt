Why the next optimization needs real FIFO checks
------------------------------------------------
The 32 registers previously scanned by Quiet are transaction-ID counters,
not FIFO status. With FIFO mode enabled, queued requests are not represented
in those counters yet; zero CMD_CTRL alone does not establish FIFO drain.
The official sequence requires testing FIFO availability before issuing
counter reads. The unchanged completed 844ec record captured NIU_CFG_0
0x16005, so this experiment supports FIFO-enabled mode, not a presumed
disabled-mode shortcut. It never changes configuration or clears counters.
