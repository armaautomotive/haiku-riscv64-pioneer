Expanding the old Q4 fragments in place would overlap their neighbors. A new
native consumer of these proposed offsets, compatible GDDR loader, bank
reservations, workspace/headroom, initialized-page proofs and real context
lifetime are still required. Thus 13 is not a hardware authorization. The
14+ aggregate excess is decisive only for the stated unchanged-allocation
assumptions, not a claim that all possible other designs are impossible.
