Dense Q8 prepared-page upload plan scan reuse, offline qualification

Before this change each DensePages::AllVerified() scanned all packed-plan
entries and also called its parent, which independently scanned all entries
against another private snapshot. At binding, the derived object now proves
its immutable snapshot equals the verified parent's snapshot; subsequent
checks still require the parent's complete live-plan scan and source/binding
checks, plus the derived object's own source and binding checks. Per-fragment
checks in ReadPages are unchanged. Snapshot mismatch fails binding.

The QGWD upload loop invokes AllVerified before and after each of 21907 spans;
for the 4678-entry plan this removes at least 204,961,892 duplicate field-group
comparisons per full upload. This is an operation count, not a measured time
or inference-speed gain. The just-completed tag142 physical timing used the old
code; the tag143 run currently underway also uses its original pinned client.
