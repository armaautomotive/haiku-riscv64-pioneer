QGWD upload-to-native-binding bridge, 2026-11-10

The QGWD binding bridge checks the same context, cookie, team, driver
generation, bank owner, ledger and hashes as the sealed upload receipt. It
also requires the exact sealed span lease, not merely an equal copy, and
rejects coexisting QGW8 or legacy receipts. At the binding boundary it
revalidates the gapped ledger and derives the logical QGW8-parent canonical
geometry used to build the QGWD worker recipes.

The actual inspection upload-control and span-service simulation now feeds
this bridge from the same context/cookie/receipt. Tests cover mismatched
owner, generation, equal-but-foreign span, competing receipts, and changed
ledger quarantine. The bridge and canonical derivation also compile with
kernel-shaped Haiku RISC-V declarations.

This remains an isolated inspection profile. It is not yet called by the
loadable native owner, does not launch the card, and establishes no inference
or performance result. A native caller must retain/quarantine ownership if
boundary revalidation faults.
