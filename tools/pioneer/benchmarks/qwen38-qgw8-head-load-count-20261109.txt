QGW8 head worker traffic: static plan analysis, 2026-11-09

The actual head job planner and BFP8 packet-shape functions were exercised
for every one of the 973 jobs, covering the full 248320-row vocabulary.
For the current loader, which fetches both operands for each 32-row band,
the plan implies per generated token:

  7760 bands x 160 K-tiles
  1241600 weight-read requests, 1350860800 weight bytes
  1241600 activation-read requests, 79462400 activation bytes
  2483200 total operand-read requests, 1430323200 operand bytes

These are on-card NoC operand reads, not host PCIe round trips, and exclude
other traffic. They are derived from planned jobs and packet shapes, not a
hardware traffic counter or measured bottleneck. Job-level parallelism,
queue overlap, request latency and the other 64 layers are not accounted for.

If the already-existing retained-input experiment were proven safe and
integrated, exactly one input load per job would mean 155680 input-read
requests and 9963520 activation bytes: 1085920 fewer requests, or 43.73%
of these operand requests, but only 69498880 fewer bytes, or 4.86% of operand
bytes. These counts assume the same job geometry and no replacement traffic;
they are not a throughput forecast. The experiment's input ownership and
lifetime remain unproven, and it must not be enabled in a native/Work image
or tested on the card solely on the basis of this calculation. See
qwen38-bfp8-input-reuse-offboard-20261107.txt for known failure limitations.
