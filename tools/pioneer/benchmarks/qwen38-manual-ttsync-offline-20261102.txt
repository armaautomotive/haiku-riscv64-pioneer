Exact implementation and limitations
------------------------------------
The separately selected full returning shader replaces the math Drain()
GPR63 ACK/poll routine with the documented ordered Manual TTSync primitive:
 discarded STORE to0xffe80004;blocking LOAD;dependent ANDI;conservative fence.
The returned load bits are UNDEFINED and are never interpreted as an ACK.
The whole real leaf is28 bytes,seven RV32 words:
 ffe807b7 00478793 0007a023 0007a703 00077713 0ff0000f 00008067
The original88-byte Drain slot is retained with60 unreachable ZERO bytes,
not60 executed NOPs. Every following function/data ABI stays within its
original mapping bounds. No writable globals,undefineds,new CSR writes,
fast-math or larger native allocation are introduced.

IMPORTANT: a missing hardware completion can block this LOAD indefinitely.
There is NO claimed local polling timeout. PCBuf OverrideEn+OverrideBusy
can bypass the completion wait. The shader DOES NOT establish a native
override-clear invariant, exclusive owner, external watchdog, source/
generation scope, STOP/HOLD proof or uncertain-resource retention. Those
must be proven in an independently guarded native integration BEFORE any
hardware use. The default/legacy selection remains OFF. New shader roles
refuse kernel/native/USER client/Work/oracle and other experimental roles.
