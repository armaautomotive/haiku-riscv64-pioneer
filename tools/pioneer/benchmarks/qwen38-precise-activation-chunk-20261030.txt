Arithmetic and boundaries
--------------------------
PioneerQ38PreciseActivationChunk preserves the current ROOT calculation:
  (gate * PioneerQ38SigmoidPrecision(gate)) * up
with two separately rounded multiplies and -ffp-contract=off. It uses the
same existing RmsDomain for each input and output: signed zero or normal
magnitudes2^-50 through65504. No reassociation, FMA, wider hidden-state
domain, old GraphSiluFast/legacy bounded-SiLU replacement, or CPU fallback.

All four caller-owned local buffers must be aligned, range-valid and
disjoint. Activation and packed output are staged, then BOTH are published
only after all256 lanes and complete quantization/packing pass. Q8_0 retains
eight FP16-rounded positive scales and ties-away semantics; Q4_K/Q6_K
retain one signed F32 scale and Q8_K ties-even/native operand rules.
The native scale domain is unchanged; a permitted F32 activation can still
be refused by the existing native packing domain. No malformed late lane
or late packing refusal leaks a partial activation or operand/scale block.

Exact isolated roles are required, including after the include guard.
Kernel/native/CPU-reference/Work/global-worker/root/fast-math/old-SiLU roles,
including defined-zero forms, are refused. These guards identify a local
arithmetic candidate, not native ownership, source or launch authority.
No existing target/default image or live driver route is changed.
