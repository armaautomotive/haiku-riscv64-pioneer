Recurrent Q8 BFP8 prepared-page adapter, offline synthetic evidence

This separate page source layers on the verified QGWD dense-Q8 source. It
routes the 1008 selected recurrent stripes through a same-source BFP8 cache,
while forwarding all other pages to the parent. It preserves the parent's
plan snapshot, addresses, bank assignments and reserved extents. It neither
creates caches nor uploads weights, owns a device, executes a model, or
enables Work/Assistant.

ASan/UBSan tests with synthetic pages passed for 5120- and 6144-wide targets,
all 1008 selected stripes, unchanged small recurrent/dense/FFN/raw routing,
padding, wrong identities, snapshot changes, overlapping output, revoked
source, and atomic failure during a batch. The old dense prepared-page suite
also passed; both page suites passed again in an independently extracted
clean source snapshot. The separate existing all-BFP8 converter suite passed
for both widths, including Q8, padded rows and source-loss handling. The
unchanged tag145 diagnostic client rebuilt to its prior SHA-256
6e36877c9daa9797547e5c066579d3149c78c805351c5607662a66e7f38db3c6.

Binding identity checks are distinct from the explicit full-plan verification
checkpoint; the latter remains required and catches changes to other parts.
No real recurrent model cache, numerical output, matrix-worker schedule,
physical device, model quality, inference speed or production safety has
been demonstrated by these tests. Further work is required for all of them.
