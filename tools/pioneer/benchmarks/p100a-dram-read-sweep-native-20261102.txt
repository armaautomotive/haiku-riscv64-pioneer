Separate strict firmware clock transport
-----------------------------------------
BlackholeDramSweepArcClock.h preserves the normal firmware application queue
protocol and only GET_AICLK/BUSY/IDLE. It requires observed unforced mode3,
idle800MHz and conservative telemetry before entering busy. Unlike the old
diagnostic transport, it has NO automatic queue/TLB restoration retries:
first uncertainty performs memory-only retention, with no subsequent IO.
Original mapping/request bytes are independently reread before modification
and after restoration. No firmware-owned response bytes or serial counters
are rewritten. Native transport methods guard the actual scope and views.
Both ARC aliases and the sweep share TLB201; the unchanged owner snapshots
after clock entry and checks the original six mapping words after leaving.
The reported firmware MHz is not yet an independent physical proof of the
device cycle-counter frequency, nor permission to publish a GB/s result.

Explicit native closure and uncertainty lifetime
-------------------------------------------------
Inner owner.closed is NOT whole-driver END. Only after the complete inner
restore and clock closure does the native controller delete all17 actual
virtual aliases, restore PCI commands/BAR/bridge fields once with readback
and a final reread, then consume the real unreserve_device return status.
Top-level complete requires all three closures. Pre-mutation refusals may
release only the reservation and are NOT successful sweeps.

Any execution/clock/VM/PCI/reservation uncertainty retains the heap context,
remaining views, backing allocation and reservation, and latches the shared
cold-recovery guard. No destructor, legacy Unmap/MmioEnd, reset, retry or
release follows retention. The FD pointer is cleared to avoid later access
to a freed cookie. Retained context is included in reload ownership/clean
detach metadata. No public execution/model/legacy selector is added.
