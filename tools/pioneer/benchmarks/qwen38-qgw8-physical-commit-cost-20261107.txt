QGW8 physical trial: state-commit scan cost candidate, 2026-11-07

This is a code-path and fixture analysis of the ACTUAL successful tag128 trial,
not a measured device profile, a demonstrated speedup, or a diagnosis of its
incorrect output. The candidate must not be deployed until numerical failure
is resolved and any optimization is independently qualified on hardware.

The preserved QGW8 ROOT build /private/tmp/pioneer-qgw8-head-root-20261107-c
has text, BF16, auxiliary, and terminal section hashes equal to RootText,
RootBf16, RootAux, and RootTerminal in the actual pinned driver program header
(header SHA-256 8ddeb0cfa01857557be9416fa61c8029040570cf67bd412e6dd1f59fdc32eecb).
Its ELF SHA-256 is 8b7bac64e64d963e729e347a209c3ca4be951992aa924ead0c48d97e0e79478f.
Disassembly of that linked ROOT shows StateSeal directly calling InputSeal,
and calling FeedbackSeal when feedback is requested; FeedbackSeal itself
calls InputSeal. These are card-side checks, NOT CPU model validation.

The unchanged CommitState source visits 48 recurrent layers, each with seven
history spans. Each span is 16896 or 17664 bytes and requires two 16 KiB-or-
smaller copy chunks. The existing actual-fragment test, run with synthetic
memory and MOCK transport, reports 1393 InputSeal calls in the final-token
case and 2786 InputSeal plus 1393 FeedbackSeal calls in the feedback case.
For the physical trial's 32 non-EOS outputs with one input, the source path
implies 31 feedback commits and one terminal commit: 87759 InputSeal calls
and 43183 FeedbackSeal calls inside CommitState alone. Each successful
InputSeal scans 12832 pairs of words; the derived count is 1126123488 pair
comparisons, plus 221096960 feedback word-pair comparisons. These figures
are derived from source/control flow and fixture counts, not instrumented
physical counters or cycles. They exclude other scans and operations. Exact
device time and the fraction of runtime attributable to them are UNKNOWN.

An earlier isolated commit-boundary alternative brackets those numeric scans
around the entire copy transaction, while retaining packet, state-descriptor,
counter and readback checks. The existing mock test reports 2/4 InputSeal
calls instead of 1393/2786, and 2 FeedbackSeal calls instead of 1393. That
alternative changes corruption-detection timing and has NOT been integrated
with QGW8, qualified on this hardware, or shown to preserve QGW8 correctness.
See qwen38-commit-boundary-seals-20261101.txt for its exact scope and limits.

The current fragment test initially failed to extract StateSeal because its
section annotation now has a profiling #if. Its runner was fixed to extract
the unique common function signature, then passed all four synthetic valid
cases and 160 injected faults. No P100A action or production firmware change
was made. Reproduce the fixture with:
