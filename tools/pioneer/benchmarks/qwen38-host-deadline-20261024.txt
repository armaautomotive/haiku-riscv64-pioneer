PioneerQ38HostDeadline records one fixed30,000,000us interval. Begin is
one-shot and rejects a start whose complete interval would overflow.
Observe permits equal timestamps but rejects backward movement,expiry
including exact equality,and observation before Begin. Any failure is
sticky;no reset/rebind/copy/move can extend an interval. The real native
owner must still obtain CPU clock observations before/after bounded card
operations and quarantine without further card IO on refusal. The helper
itself never reads time,changes a device or grants cleanup authority.
