OFFLINE signed integer decoding prerequisite, October21 2026.
NO image/driver/native selector or throughput qualification.

PioneerQ38SignedMagnitude2.h replaces eight register-only TC->SM integer
operations with two: SFPABS INT VC0->VD2, then SFPSETSGN VC2->VD0 REG.
Crucial: SETSGN takes magnitude FROM VC2 and ORIGINAL sign FROM DEST0,
not the reverse. Original0 must survive the ABS. Q8 -128..127 and Q6
-32..31 are inside the domain; INT32_MIN is explicitly outside it.
No FPU approximation,conditional-state change,Dst/store/source/NoC/CSR
or ownership change. Not integrated into any compiled peer yet.
