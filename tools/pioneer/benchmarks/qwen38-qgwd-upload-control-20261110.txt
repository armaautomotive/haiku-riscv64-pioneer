QGWD separate upload-control inspection, 2026-11-10

The isolated QGWD control handler now creates and seals the gapped-ledger
receipt under the same simulated cookie, team, generation and bank owner as
the actual tracked span service. It rejects old-format packets, a foreign
owner/team, simultaneous older receipts and repeated BEGIN. An actual span
service call was exercised with simulated RV32 transport; the remaining
21,906 spans were filled directly into the synthetic ledger before SEAL.

The test also checked that losing the BEGIN or SEAL reply, tampering with a
reserved gap, or changing generation before SEAL retains/quarantines the
owner. In the inspection profile both older controls reject BEGIN while the
QGWD receipt exists; existing QGW8 control is also regression tested.

Command:
  python3 tools/pioneer/tests/run_pioneer_q38_qgwd_upload_control_tests.py ABS_FAST_IMAGE

This handler is NOT installed in a driver and does not dispatch a real ioctl.
Reciprocal exclusion has only been compiled and exercised in the isolated
profile, not wired into a live driver. Native owner/manifest integration,
hardware execution, model quality and inference speed remain unverified.
