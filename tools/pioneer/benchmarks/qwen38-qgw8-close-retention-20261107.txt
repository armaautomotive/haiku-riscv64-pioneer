QGW8 receipt retention on descriptor close
===========================================

The actual ReleaseResidentCookie path previously retained an uncertain
tracked upload only when the legacy hybrid receipt was present. A QGW8-only
receipt could therefore fall through to normal session cleanup, even though
the QGW8 client deliberately closes without END after uncertain operations.
That was unsafe and the synthetic user transport had masked the mismatch.

When the QGW8 span role is selected, the actual close handler now retains the
owner for either receipt, including the ambiguous case where both are present.
The existing legacy-only build behavior is unchanged. The simulated OS test
exercises the actual extracted handler for QGW8-only, both-receipt, ordinary
and failed-lock cases; it checks that tracked close does not invoke END or I/O.
The separate existing legacy close and upload-control tests also pass.

This does not prove real-device cleanup, output correctness or performance.
No driver was installed and no P100A operation or recovery was attempted.
