Change and intended benefit
--------------------------
The measured latest ROOT preparation phase was 30.46% of elapsed cycles.
BfPrepare first admits every worker, runs and closes the BF16 producers,
then used to audit the producer-unused workers again. Run already performs
matrix Gather or a full idle audit on every worker before Scatter.

The new explicit --deferred-prepare-audit BUILD omits ONLY that extra
pre-matrix idle audit. A private 52-byte stack coverage ledger requires all
119 final closures, exactly once in their proper matrix/idle partition,
before Scatter. It does not manufacture a source, numerical or lifetime
receipt. Initial all-worker admission, genuine producer proofs, transfer
guards, worker-private pre-request admission and final closure remain.

This deliberately changes fault-observation timing: an idle fault arising
after initial admission may be detected later, during matrix Gather or the
final idle audit. No claim of identical intermediate observations is made.
Trusted worker instructions remain a requirement, not something this ledger
proves. The real connected execution and native ownership are NOT qualified.

Source-derived traffic budget, NOT measured time or packet telemetry
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The omitted idle success path contains at least 15 Transfer calls:
Wait has 3 per successful poll, BootSeal 1, ClosedJob 3, counters 8.
Their requested payload sums to 684 bytes; tiny reads still incur control
and packet overhead. Extra Wait polls would increase the old cost.
K5120: 99 omitted idle audits; K17408: 51 omitted idle audits.
For the model's 64 layers with two K5120 and one K17408 Q4 projections,
this removes at least 239,040 such serial control requests per token,
10,900,224 requested payload bytes. This is a source-path calculation,
NOT a physical transaction counter or a model speedup prediction.

Even eliminating the ENTIRE measured preparation category would only give
about 1.44x speedup if every other cost remained fixed. This smaller change
cannot by itself close the roughly 122x gap from the best rate to 20 TPS.
No additional model trial was run merely to repeat the known slow baseline.
