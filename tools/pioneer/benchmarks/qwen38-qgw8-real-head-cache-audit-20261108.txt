QGW8 output-head cache and job layout: real-data OFFLINE check
=============================================================

The first physical tag128 27B run chose the wrong first token. While its
follow-on hardware diagnostic could not be reached after a power outage, a
read-only Mac-side check compared the complete prepared head with fresh
conversion from the independently authenticated original model. This is not
a P100A result, inference run, numerical model-quality pass or speed test.

The original 18 GB GGUF matched SHA-256
c600de0300ae8a0eb3a6c0b8b5561b8b96f16bd2c863c2a66c42de29d391a747.
All seven existing Mac BFP8 output-head fragments were opened using their
recorded host converter identity, verified by the cache reader, and compared
page-by-page against the freshly generated BFP8 data, including zero padding:

 bank  pages   rows
 0     47090   35456
 1     47090   35456
 2     47175   35520
 3     47175   35520
 4     47090   35456
 5     47090   35456
 6     47090   35456

All 329800 pages / 1350860800 bytes matched byte-for-byte. Actual QGW8 job
construction also produced 973 jobs covering all 248320 rows exactly once;
each job's bank route and byte offset matched its cached-stripe coordinate.
The checker opened the model and caches read-only; it did not repair, rewrite,
upload or execute them. The actual checker process exited 0. Its binary SHA-256
is 58efb5f51a9a12ba6027af5635498be4b743ba6146e94cad1868930128cf229a.

The first attempt used the Haiku converter identity against the Mac cache and
correctly refused a cache miss; it did not change any files. The successful
attempt used the documented distinct Mac fingerprint. A previous separate
audit compared the seven Mac and Haiku cache payloads by full SHA-256, as
recorded in qwen38-qgw8-extra-weights-mac-haiku-20261107.txt. The present
result does not recheck Haiku after the outage and does not prove what was
uploaded, retained in card memory, or consumed by the actual matrix engines.
Fresh conversion and cache construction share code, so this is not an
independent validation of the conversion mathematics. It narrows one possible
host cache/job-layout cause but does not identify the incorrect-token cause.

The offline checker build, incompatible-role and invalid-input tests passed.
The existing head plan's synthetic 973-job coverage and RV32 compile tests
also passed. No Work-mode validation or reference inference was added.
