-----------------------

the prior generation-profile run measured 0.172871238..0.172874445.
These are single runs. The displayed ranges bound timestamp observations,
NOT run-to-run variability or confidence intervals. The tiny differences
do not establish a repeatable or practical speedup. Do not replace the
stable engine, install this experiment in Assistant, or repeat the long
test as a substitute for a stronger idea. The candidate is REJECTED as a
meaningful performance improvement; its isolated code/evidence remain.
