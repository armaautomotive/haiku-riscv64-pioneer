Three recurrent projection roles with real-weight worker simulation

The private QKV exporter authenticated the pinned full model and prepared
three 32-row bands of blk.0.attn_qkv.weight (tensor 5; rows 0, 5120, 10208).
All 480 cache tiles matched fresh conversion, and an independent integer
decoder/arithmetic checker matched all 96 expected outputs. Synthetic
invalid-input and role-exclusion tests passed.

The compiled producer and matrix-worker/leaf images were then simulated for
three real-weight samples each of attention gate (role 5, K5120), QKV
(role 6, K5120), and recurrent output (role 13, K6144). For every sample,
the producer generated the full input and all 119 scheduled matrix-worker
identities matched the independently checked 32-word reference. Each worker
and each band received the same sample, not its own distinct model rows.
The worker/leaf ELF identities and private sample files were pinned and
section extraction verified; see qwen38-recurrent-out-real-worker-simulation.

These are simulated worker instructions, FPU and NoC only. No full recurrent
layer/ROOT lifecycle, complete 27B inference, hardware speed, or output
quality is established. The independent width-6144 hardware diagnostic was
running separately when this report was recorded.
