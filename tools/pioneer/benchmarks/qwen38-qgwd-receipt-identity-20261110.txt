QGWD receipt per-access identity check, 2026-11-10

QGWD now has the same distinction already used by the QGW8 receipt: Matches
checks the bound sealed context, FD, hashes/ledger pointers, team, generation,
and bank proof without rescanning every page. Revalidate continues to scan the
gapped ledger and quarantine a mismatch. A native implementation must still
perform full revalidation at the bind, manifest, and pre-launch boundaries;
the cheap identity check is not source authentication or device readback.

Synthetic tests check mismatched identities, demonstrate that Matches alone
does not detect a modified hash, and verify that manifest revalidation then
detects and quarantines the changed ledger. The method also compiled against
Haiku's RISC-V kernel-shaped ABI in the non-loadable profile.

This prepares a bounded per-access scope for future QGWD native integration.
It is not yet used by the live driver, and no speedup has been measured.
