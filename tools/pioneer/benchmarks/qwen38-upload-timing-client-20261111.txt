QGWD upload timing client, build and simulation only
===================================================

An opt-in tag142 diagnostic client separates wall time in prepared-page
reads from wall time inside span-upload ioctls. It records byte and call
counts and a sticky validity flag for clock or counter errors. These are
host wall-clock intervals, not raw PCIe bandwidth, DMA utilization or a
direct measurement of individual kernel/device phases. Other startup and
setup time is outside those sums. Model preflight precedes the old overall
upload/start timer and is also outside these sums.

The timing wrapper forwards the real prepared-page provider and does not
change upload packets or driver code. Output aliases of the wrapper and
its counters are rejected. A synthetic full QGWD upload checked all 3551
fragments, 21907 packets and 21094223872 bytes through the actual uploader,
with exact payload/packet verification; the clock arithmetic test includes
backward/zero clocks, counter overflow, failed reads and alias refusal.
