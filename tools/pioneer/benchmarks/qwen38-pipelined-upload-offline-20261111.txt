QGWF prepared-page/upload overlap experiment, offline only

The measured tag142 run spent 1213.943 s in prepared-page reads and
1237.065 s in upload IOCTLs. This distinct userspace experiment uses two
bounded 1 MiB span packets: a single worker prepares the next packet from
the verified cache while the original owner thread performs the current
upload IOCTL. It is an opportunity for overlap, not a measured speedup; disk,
CPU, memory and card contention can prevent ideal overlap.

All device calls, progress and ownership remain on the original thread.
Only one preparation job may be outstanding. Before touching Pages again,
the owner waits for that job; source verification continues before and after
each upload. A failed read, uncertain IOCTL or malformed reply stops without
retry and joins the worker before buffers can be released. This code does not
make driver END, close or recovery safe. The experimental role excludes Work,
CPU validation, kernel builds and the old shared upload-timing instrumentation.

Synthetic ASan/UBSan tests passed: a complete 21907-span same-FD session with
17 synthetic outputs; failed START, bad feedback, bad frontier; page failure
before the first IOCTL and during a subsequent preparation; failed IOCTL and
malformed reply. The fake driver verified it ran on the owner thread, and a
barrier demonstrated producer progress while that fake call was active.
The worker's rapid reuse, exception handling and pending-job join also passed.
These tests do not prove hardware safety or performance.
