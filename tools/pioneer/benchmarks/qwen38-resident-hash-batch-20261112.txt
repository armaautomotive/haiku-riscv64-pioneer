Resident page-hash batch experiment, 2026-11-12

The span-upload service records the existing non-cryptographic per-page FNV
hash after a successful transfer. Its hash definition, per-page publication
order, ownership checks, upload proof and Work/strict distinction are not
changed. The candidate interleaves four independent 4 KiB page hashes, with
the existing scalar hash for trailing pages. This is a loading-path change,
not an inference-kernel or quality change.

After the protected P100A benchmark had closed, the standalone CPU-only
candidate was copied to the Pioneer and SHA-256 checked:
a7852d232cd6153e092878a82ab94e2e9bc9eae8c97019f55389f2fdf57f9797.
It produced the original exact hashes for 512 synthetic pages. Three
64 MiB cache-hot synthetic runs, scalar first each time, measured respectively
226734000/90458000, 168410000/90476000 and 168418000/90494000 ns
for scalar/four-page hashing. These are CPU microbenchmarks with a 16 KiB
working set, not a model upload, accelerator bandwidth or tokens/sec result;
order and caching effects are not controlled. No device was accessed.

The integrated implementation was then checked against the original hash
under ASan/UBSan; strict and separate Work-mode actual span-service mocks
passed, including 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 256-page requests, exact hash
publication, refusal and cleanup paths. The resident-store tests passed.
The separate QGWD tracked-receipt span simulation also passed in strict and
fast modes, including ordering and quarantine checks.
