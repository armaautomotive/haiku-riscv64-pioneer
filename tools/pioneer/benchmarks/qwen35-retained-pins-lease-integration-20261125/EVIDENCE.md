# Retained pin and controller lease integration witness

The new compile-only scope nests retained card and root pin operations around the retained SG2042 configuration lease. It parks the lease before the pin callbacks return, including their removal postchecks, and never releases any of these objects. This witness is not exported or connected to a recovery path.

`sh tools/pioneer/tests/run_pioneer_retained_recovery_scope_tests.sh` passed with ASan and UBSan. The test extracts actual device-manager pin, node lifetime, and controller lease method bodies and runs them with simulated nodes, locks, threads and controller: a pending retry parks the lease and wakes a competing configuration caller with `B_DEV_NOT_READY`, then the same ticket resumes on another simulated thread. It also verifies that a reused pin object is rejected, stale generation prevents entry, removal during the operation retains the parked lease, and completed or faulted scopes reject further work. Four unguarded or qualification build combinations were rejected. `sh tools/pioneer/tests/run_pioneer_device_manager_pin_tests.sh` also passed.

Test teardown supplies the release flag solely for simulated objects; it is not evidence of safe hardware release. The integration has no independent proof that the pins, controller and device match, no exclusion of firmware, DMA or MMIO, no protection against physical removal during a callback, no owner/thread lifetime proof and no validated real recovery ticket. A failed park may leave the lease active. Safe teardown and thread exit handling are unresolved. No card was accessed; no hardware recovery, valid model output, or decode rate is demonstrated.
