Next engineering gate
---------------------
The candidate is not ready to install. Under its candidate macro, native
startup still invokes `PioneerQ38TokenStartupPlan::Build(...)` without the
`redistributedGdn=true` argument, so the scattered page allocator/map is not
selected for that token. Even after selecting the map, the
97,484,800-byte page journal is not instantiated or bound to the native
session. Correct integration must preserve every page's arbitrary preexisting
data across launch, commit/replay the cache only at the outer model-token
boundary, and restore on every successful close without exposing partial
state after failure. A naive full restore cannot fit the existing 300 ms
`RestoreOriginalL1` deadline; it needs bounded/batched transport and explicit
fault-retention semantics before hardware testing.

The live machine has 64 CPUs and full RAM with stable041 restored. No current
all-card Qwen request has returned a valid speed result. Last qualified
full-Q4 result remains approximately 0.1728 tokens/sec; 20 tokens/sec remains
unmet.
