Recurrent converted-weight ownership transfer, offline inspection

Added a simulated transfer of a sealed recurrent upload receipt to a fresh
request context while retaining the same span, FD, team and generation. The
transfer checks disjoint ledger storage, matching DRAM identity and routes,
complete destination ledger integrity and equality before changing pointers.
Rejected transfers leave the original binding intact. This is groundwork for
reusing resident converted weights; it does not itself retain real card RAM.

ASan/UBSan exercised the full synthetic 21,475-span ledger and two successive
transfers after retiring old arrays, bank objects and proof objects. Foreign
FD, generation and span, overlapping arrays, a spurious page, altered hash
and changed route were refused. The existing quarantine, workspace and role
exclusion regressions also passed. All data, spans and bank endpoints in this
test were simulated.

The actual exclusive lock, physical reservation continuity, ownership of both
contexts during failures, copyout handling, device bytes, model execution and
performance remain unproven. This inspection role is excluded from the real
driver, Work and CPU-reference modes; no model was run or benchmarked.
