Why this test is needed
-----------------------
The earlier physical version3 queued reader improved one bank/one worker's
device-cycle count by2.365x at depth16 vs depth1. Its mcycle frequency is
not independently calibrated. Adding seven isolated rates is NOT measured
aggregate bandwidth. New version4 runs seven bank readers concurrently and
uses ONE coordinator clock from BEFORE the first release to receipt of ALL
seven completion flags. Reader clocks are never summed or assumed aligned.
That interval includes seven sequential acknowledged release packets,launch
skew and completion polling. It excludes upload,post-run readout/restoration.

Fixed topology:coordinator(1,2),readers(2,2)..(7,2) and(10,2),NoC0. Each
reader binds one of seven DISTINCT firmware-derived bank routes in its DATA
job and reads the same8MiB source window at0x04000000. Up to16 disjoint16KiB
returns occupy0x90000..0xcffff per reader. Source is READ-only;coordination
writes target only owned peer L1 Start words,never GDDR or host memory.
Nonposted,non-inline0x212 writes request real ACKs. No broadcast,linked VC,
atomics,counter reset,forced clock or tensor instruction is introduced.

Version4 DATA is32 words with role,nonce,sweep count,depth and seven routes.
The entire shape is checked BEFORE MMIO/CSR. Actual local NODE_ID/shape
registers must match the selected role before readiness and after execution;
coordinator rechecks its identity before EVERY packet. It reads each actual
peer job and exact matching ready nonce before the common start. Reports
are published before done;root refuses mismatched roles/nonces/counts/routes,
reserved words or nonmonotonic time. Seven after-ACK release timestamps are
retained on the coordinator clock to expose skew. Byte totals are64bit:
at256 sweeps,7*2GiB is15,032,385,536B,not a wrapped32bit count. This repeats
seven8MiB windows;it is NOT15GB of distinct memory or a full-capacity test.

The common interval is a measured envelope only AFTER actual native use.
One reader per bank on NoC0 does NOT by itself prove saturation or peak
bandwidth:later scaling/more readers/NoC1 may still change the plateau.
Do not use this prototype or simulated clock values to certify the20tok/s
target as possible/impossible,or the card as authentic/faulty.
