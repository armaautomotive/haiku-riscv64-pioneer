Native BFP4 card-cycle timing: OFFBOARD preparation only
=======================================================
November 6, 2026. This is not a physical timing or model speed measurement.

A distinct tag119 synthetic diagnostic adds two bounded high/low/high
read-only mcycle samples around each complete K-width BFP4 band. Its 64-byte
record occupies the bottom of the already snapshotted private 8KiB stack
span; the interpreter verifies the actual linked stack remains above it.
Two passes return all 64 normal mathematical outputs plus both timing
records. The owner requires valid ordered clock samples and unchanged timing
at END; unavailable or altered records, an old marker or an uncertain state
leave ownership quarantined without a retry or automatic cleanup.

Executed locally:
* run_pioneer_native_bfp4_timed_image_tests.py: three widths, MMIO and
  direct-issue variants, valid clock including low-word rollover and bounded
  unavailable clock, actual RV32 instructions and ideal arithmetic observer.
* run_pioneer_native_bfp4_timed_owner_tests.py: all three MMIO images,
  rounded-arithmetic predictions, complete simulated restore, invalid and
  altered clocks, wrong marker, bad PC and timed/untimed role separation.
* pioneer_native_bfp_band_client_test.py --bfp4-timed: 224 fake-endpoint
  cases including every RUN/END result word, invalid clock records and all
  16 timing words modified at END. Existing BFP4 and BFP8 client suites
  each passed 200 fake cases, and the untimed BFP4 owner tests passed.
No hardware was opened by these checks.

Comparison boundary
-------------------
The earlier K5120 Q4 diagnostic recorded 690318 and 690195 card cycles for
32 outputs. It includes on-card GDDR loading/NoC, unpack and compute. The
present BFP4 candidate's clock interval begins only after the diagnostic has
staged the entire synthetic band in local L1: it includes BFP4 setup, per-tile
unpack/compute and drains, but excludes GDDR/NoC loading and conversion. A
timed result can be compared as a preliminary lower-scope component, not as
an apples-to-apples inference speedup or a prediction of tokens/sec. Real
model quality, resident GDDR integration and end-to-end timing remain open.
