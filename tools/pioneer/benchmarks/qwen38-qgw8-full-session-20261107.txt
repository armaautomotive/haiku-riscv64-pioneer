QGW8 upload-to-token session, synthetic only
============================================

The existing full-session lifecycle now has a distinct QGW8 inspection role
using the QGW8 page uploader and receipt rather than the older hybrid layout.
It checks the full-generation capability before beginning a bank session or
uploading weights, holds the same descriptor through upload and START,
OBSERVE, HOLD, READ and FINISH, and closes only after the driver's explicit
finish. Uncertain operations close once and retain/quarantine the owner;
they do not retry, reset, or issue BANK_END.

The QGW8 inspection protocol uses its own synthetic identity. Role guards
reject legacy or physical driver/Work selectors, CPU-reference modes, and
late changes between upload-only and full-session roles. A Haiku RISC-V
compile-only witness instantiates the actual prepared QGW8 page source and
full-session types with a transport that always refuses to open.

Synthetic tests cover the complete 22035-span QGW8 upload and two returned
token records using one owner/descriptor, refusal of unsupported capability
before bank BEGIN, and owner retention after a failed START or malformed
token record. The old hybrid full-session tests and Haiku candidate-client
cross-builds also pass. These are synthetic device replies, not a model token
generated on the P100A; no output quality or tokens/second is established.

The QGW8 control and session are still non-loadable inspection profiles. A
physical QGW8 driver/owner, safe hardware qualification, real inference and
Assistant integration remain unfinished.
