These are deliberately different from the Haiku-target fingerprints. They
are not Haiku-qualified and cannot simply be substituted in the target
preflight. Verification reused the same implementation; it establishes
internal cache integrity on these reads, not independent conversion quality
or cross-platform equivalence. The existing 1071 Haiku BFP4 fragments can
be compared by logical entry and verified payload before deciding whether any
future migration is safe; the remaining 42 and seven head fragments still
need independent qualification. No cache was installed on the Pioneer, no
accelerator computation was issued, and no inference or speed was measured.

The current verified Linux SSH identity reports the prior Linux boot ID and
the P100A PCI function is enumerated without a Linux driver. This is not a
new recovery epoch or proof the accelerator is safe to execute. The existing
driver recovery guard and unresolved Haiku-volume durability observations
remain unchanged.
