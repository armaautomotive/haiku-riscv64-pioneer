QGWE combined dense-Q8 / batched-BFP8 worker: BUILD ONLY
========================================================

This isolated worker combines the current QGWD dense-Q8/SrcA worker and
the earlier QGWA per-band batched BFP8 input leaf. It uses new QGWE/QGOE
boot and job witnesses, so neither existing QGWD nor QGWA controller should
be assumed compatible. It cannot be selected by the native driver or Work.

The unchanged QGWD worker rebuilt to its pinned ELF SHA-256:

The builder's section bounds and instruction audits passed. The new global
worker section is 13180 bytes; cached FPU is 13756 bytes. Compile-only role
and identity exclusions passed; the prior dense-Q8 layout tests also passed.

and FFN instruction simulator with mocked NoC/Tensix: head bands 1/2/3/8,

are synthetic transport and instruction counts, not card timings. Layer
history was injected and the dense-layer arithmetic is not covered here.

producer-to-matrix handoff also passed for both pinned workers with mocked
