Recurrent-layer Q8 to BFP8 matrix geometry, offline only

Current QGWD selects the converted BFP8 producer for dense-layer Q/G, K and V
and for the existing FFN/head paths. This proposal adds a distinct data-only
layout for recurrent layers' large Q8 roles 5, 6 and 13: respectively
5120x6144, 5120x10240 and 6144x5120. The two Q8 48-row projections per
recurrent layer remain in their original encoding; partial-tile policy and
their execution are not implemented by this proposal.

The canonical source plan covers 48 recurrent layers. The isolated test
accepted 1008 stripes and 1,032,192 rows, verified the existing BFP8 tile
geometry for both 5120- and 6144-wide inputs, rejected altered type/row
metadata, and confirmed that all other parts, bank assignments, offsets and
used extents remain unchanged. The converted shapes leave 346,030,080 bytes
of holes within prior reserved extents; those bytes are NOT reclaimed VRAM.
ASan/UBSan geometry and native/Work/reference role exclusion tests passed.

This is only a proposed memory layout: it neither creates caches nor
converts or uploads actual model weights, and no worker or driver selects
it. BFP8 is a requantization and needs numerical/quality qualification.
The required next steps include authenticated cache/page production,
physical layout and ownership proof, new projection schedules and worker
execution, tests against an independent reference, then guarded on-card
measurement. There is no hardware, model-quality, token-rate or speedup claim.
