QGW8 head-band diagnostic 130: isolated DATA and simulated lease only
====================================================================

A distinct compile-time identity selects the C64 learned-band journal with
the private real output-head fixture. It has tag 2026110800010130 and opcode
0x9500; the original 81/84 tags and selection remain separate. The fixed C64
on-card image still emits the existing 84 arithmetic marker: the new tag,
opcode, namespace and image-layout role distinguish the diagnostic, not a
claim that a new hardware image was executed. Work and CPU-reference build
roles are explicitly refused. No production default selects this role.

The compile-only role test passed legacy81, legacy84 and head130 identities,
12 refused incompatible/late selections, including the exact kernel tag guard.
With the three private, pinned real-head bands and the pinned C64 image,
the actual shared lease ran under synthetic I/O twice: once in legacy84 and
once in new head130 selection. Each run produced 192 exact expected words and
passed 251 injected failure/admission/close cases, with first-uncertainty
retention and no simulated retry/reset/cleanup. Two additional compile checks
refused changing the role after the lease was first included. These results
are offboard simulation, not actual PCI/VM/FD authority or a hardware test.
